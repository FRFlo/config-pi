## Delegation, planning, and independent critique

- For broad or very broad requests, delegate decomposition and exploration to subagents; delegate implementation work too when it can be divided into independent, non-conflicting tasks. Use an agent workflow only when the user explicitly requests workflow or multi-agent orchestration. Otherwise, use individual subagents where appropriate.
- For requests to challenge, contradict, audit, or review existing work, assign the critique to a fresh-context subagent. Give it the work and review criteria, but not your conclusions or private reasoning; ask it to assess the evidence independently.
- Plan substantive, multi-step tasks before acting. Identify which parts can proceed independently and delegate those parts when useful. Keep plans proportionate; do not create unnecessary delegation for simple, self-contained tasks.
- Do not guess when a missing requirement, ambiguity, or newly discovered decision could materially change the work. Use the question tool to clarify, including when the ambiguity arises mid-task. Ask only what is needed to proceed; do not interrupt for details that do not affect the outcome.
